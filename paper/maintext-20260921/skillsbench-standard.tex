\subsection{Skill Utility on Standard SkillsBench Tasks}
We first measure the task-level utility of each skill-evolution pipeline relative to skill-free execution. The standard-task evaluations cover SkillEvolver, SPARK, and CoEvoSkills. For each evaluation, we compare the method condition with its baseline on the same tasks and average the task-level reward differences:
\begin{equation}
\widehat{\Delta}_m = \frac{1}{|S_m|}\sum_{i\in S_m}\left(r_{i,m}-r_{i,\varnothing}\right),
\end{equation}
where $S_m$ is the evaluated task set, $r_{i,m}\in[0,1]$ is the method-condition reward, and $r_{i,\varnothing}$ is its skill-free counterpart. Table~\ref{tab:skillsbench-standard-utility} reports mean rewards on a 0--100 scale, paired gains in percentage points, and the numbers of improved, regressed, and tied tasks.

All five evaluations show positive mean paired gains. SkillEvolver gains 25.61 and 25.02 points in its two 81-task evaluations, while CoEvoSkills gains 10.12 points over 86 tasks. SkillEvolver's baseline mean rises by 7.12 points between evaluations, while its paired gain changes by only 0.59 points. Its two evaluations thus produce similar average gains despite shifts in absolute reward.

SPARK's two server-native evaluations cover the same 84 tasks. Rep01 increases mean reward from 17.82 to 19.66, a gain of 1.84 points; Rep02 increases it from 14.37 to 20.81, a gain of 6.45 points. The positive direction is shared across both evaluations, while the magnitude varies by 4.61 points. Each gain is calculated against the baseline from its own evaluation.

Positive averages coexist with uneven task-level outcomes. Across the five evaluations, 9--27 tasks improve, 3--9 regress, and 48--67 tie. Unchanged rewards account for most tasks in every evaluation, so the aggregate gains are concentrated in a subset of tasks. This pattern motivates examining which improvements persist when skills are redeployed or task requirements change.

Appendix~\ref{app:skillsbench-records} records execution settings, task coverage, and recovery accounting for each evaluation.
