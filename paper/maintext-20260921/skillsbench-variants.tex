\subsection{SkillsBench Task Variations}
We examine how recorded skill utility varies across task changes using 222 SkillsBench variants and four conditions: no skill, SkillEvolver, SPARK, and CoEvoSkills. The collection distinguishes multi-axis perturbations (P1), structural changes (P2), and coupled changes to task requirements (P3). Table~\ref{tab:variant-examples} presents selected historical outcomes that illustrate differences across variants and methods.

The direction of the observed difference changes within a task family. On Exoplanet P1 and P3, SkillEvolver receives reward 1 while the skill-free condition receives 0; on P2, the skill-free condition receives 1 and all three method conditions receive 0. Other examples distinguish shared success from method-specific failure: all three methods receive 1 on Earthquake plate P3, compared with 0 without a skill, whereas SPARK alone receives 0 on Financial modeling P2. These cases identify task changes under which the observed utility of a skill differs, motivating the parent--final interventions used to investigate retained behavior.

The full inventory reports 838 historical task--condition entries and 48 recovery entries separately by execution protocol, with two further entries excluded for unavailable transcription service. Appendix~\ref{app:skillsbench-records} documents the exclusions, five historical outcome conflicts, and three file-reading reevaluations.
